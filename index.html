Guardrails in this prototype

Advice boundary
Questions like “should I buy…?” get facts and risks, not a recommendation.
Forecast boundary
No price or exchange-rate predictions. Only what moved and what sources say.
Privacy
Questions never need personal IDs. A 12-digit ID or card-like number is flagged and not processed.
Data age
Every answer shows how old its data is and turns amber, then red, as it ages.
No source, no answer
Out-of-scope questions get a clear “not verified” and links to KASE sections.
Calculated numbers are labelled
Anything we derived (for example, YTD index change) is marked “calc” with its inputs.
